\section*{AI use statement}
\label{sec:ai-use}
We used AI tools for literature retrieval, drafting and revising text, and implementing the measurement and evaluation methods.
The tools also assisted with experiment planning, aggregation and interpretation of recorded results, developing and checking mathematical arguments, and preparation of tables and figures.
AI-generated programs are also part of the compilation method evaluated in Section~\ref{sec:measure}.
The authors are responsible for checking the sources, results, mathematical claims, and final manuscript.

\section*{Ethics statement}
\label{sec:ethics}
The study uses benchmark applications, an Android emulator, desktop applications in a Linux container, and locally hosted web applications.
The arrival experiments use public event logs.
The study does not involve recruiting human participants or executing generated actions on users' personal accounts.
Our cost bound does not ensure task correctness, so applying the method to consequential actions would require checks appropriate to those actions.

\section*{Reproducibility statement}
\label{sec:reproducibility}
The code and retained records are at \url{https://anonymous.4open.science/r/computer-use}.
Appendix~\ref{app:measurement-details} describes the task families, recorded model identifiers, cost calibrations, and verification procedures.
Section~\ref{sec:guarantee} states and proves the cost bound, and Appendix~\ref{app:protocol-details} gives the complete execution order and reference calculations.
Appendix~\ref{app:simulation-details} specifies the experimental grids, arrival logs, cost substitutions, paired outcomes, and confidence interval calculation.
Appendix~\ref{app:live} describes the live GUI study and its charge records.
The artifact includes measurement readers, versioned experiment entry points, paired results, table renderers, and manifests identifying their inputs.
