\caption{PACE's measurement and online compilation protocols.
(a) Validation selects a program, while fresh deployment inputs and matched agent replays evaluate serving costs and task success.
Repeated compilations check variation in cost and outcome.
Source runs are charged once as service, and all compilation charges are retained.
(b) PACE serves the current task before considering compilation for later tasks.
Measured costs, past arrivals, and compilation outcomes inform the price test, including the price of failed attempts.
The two shield checks share one cumulative budget relative to agent execution under the stated cost model.
Program service extracts inputs before execution, falls back on detected failure, and invalidates programs after detected breaks.}
