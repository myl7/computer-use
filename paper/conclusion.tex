\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\looseness=-1
PACE measures successful and failed compilation costs and per-use savings, then uses these profiles to make cost-aware compilation proposals.
Execution and compilation checks use the budget from observed arrivals to enforce $K_t\leq(1+\epsilon)A_t$ under valid action-cost bounds.
For successful compilation attempts, estimated payback counts excluding source agent runs are $2$--$16$ uses.
In simulations using recorded task arrivals, PACE saves $17.3\%$ relative to ReAct, $24.9\%$ relative to the AutoRPA adaptation, and $17.3\%$ relative to the ToolPro adaptation ($\epsilon=0.25$).
Ablations separate the effects of the savings test, failed-attempt cost, and the cost budget.
