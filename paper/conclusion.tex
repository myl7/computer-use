\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Compiling a computer-use task can make later runs cheap while incurring substantial and uncertain initial spend.
PACE measures both costs and decides when to compile under an agent-relative budget.
With valid action-cost bounds, the budget limits cost after every arrival, including failed compilations and streams that end before payback.
The log simulations average $0.781$ of agent-only cost, while ablations separate the effects of compilation selection, failed-attempt prices, and cost protection.
