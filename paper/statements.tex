\subsection*{AI use statement}
\label{sec:ai-use}
\looseness=-1
We used AI tools for finding relevant literature, drafting and revising text, and implementing the measurement and evaluation methods.
The tools also helped plan experiments, combine and interpret recorded results, develop and check mathematical arguments, and prepare tables and figures.
AI-generated programs are also part of the compilation method evaluated in Section~\ref{sec:measure}.
We have reviewed all AI-assisted work.
We take responsibility for the final content of this paper.

\subsection*{Ethics statement}
\label{sec:ethics}
The study uses benchmark applications, an Android emulator, desktop applications in a Linux container, and locally hosted web applications.
The arrival experiments use public event logs.
The study does not involve recruiting human participants or executing generated actions on users' personal accounts.
Our cost bound does not ensure task correctness, so applying the method to actions with important real-world effects would require checks appropriate to those actions.

\subsection*{Reproducibility statement}
\label{sec:reproducibility}
\looseness=-1
The code and saved records are at \url{https://anonymous.4open.science/r/computer-use}.
Appendix~\ref{app:measurement-details} describes the task families, recorded model identifiers, cost calculations, and verification procedures.
Section~\ref{sec:guarantee} states and proves the cost bound, and Appendix~\ref{app:protocol-details} gives the complete execution order and reference calculations.
Appendix~\ref{app:simulation-details} specifies the experimental grids, arrival logs, estimates of missing costs, paired outcomes, and confidence interval calculation.
Appendix~\ref{app:live} describes the live GUI study and its charge records.
The released files include scripts for reading measurements, running experiments, and creating tables, along with paired results and lists of the inputs used.
