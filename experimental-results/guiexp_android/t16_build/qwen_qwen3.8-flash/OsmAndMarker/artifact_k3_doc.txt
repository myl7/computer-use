Open OsmAnd and wait for the map screen.
Tap Search in the map toolbar.
Tap the search text field.
If the search field contains previous text, clear it.
Type the entire {location} verbatim into the single search field.
Do not reformat, split, round, or convert {location}.
Wait for the search results list.
If no result appears, tap Increase search radius until a result appears.
Tap the first search result row, even if its label is formatted differently.
Wait for the place panel or location context menu to open.
Do not tap the favorites star.
Scroll the place panel if needed until the Marker control is visible.
Tap the Marker control on the place panel.
Do not look for a confirmation dialog; the marker is stored on the tap.
Return to the map.
