Open OsmAnd and wait for the map screen.
Tap the Search control on the map screen.
Tap the search field on the search screen.
If the search field is prefilled, tap Clear and make it empty.
Type {location} verbatim into the search field.
Do not reformat, round, or split {location}.
Wait for the search results list to appear.
If no results appear, tap Increase search radius and wait.
Tap the first result row; it may show {location} or a formatted coordinate title.
Wait for the place panel to open.
Do not tap the favorites star.
Tap the Marker control on the place panel.
The marker is saved immediately.
No confirmation dialog is needed.
Return to the map screen.
