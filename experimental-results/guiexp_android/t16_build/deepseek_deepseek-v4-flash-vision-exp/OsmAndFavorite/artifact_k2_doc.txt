1. Open OsmAnd to the map screen.
2. Tap the Search button on the map screen.
3. On the search screen, type {location} into the search field (hint "Type to search all"), exactly as given — the field accepts both place names and coordinates, so never reformat or round it.
4. Tap the first search result that matches {location} (do not tap "SHOW ON MAP" or the map surface).
5. On the place panel that appears, tap the FAVORITES star icon (not the marker icon next to it).
6. In the Add Favorite dialog, leave the name field as the app proposes (do not clear or edit it).
7. Tap Save to confirm the dialog.
