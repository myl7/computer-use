Open OsmAnd.
On the map screen, tap the Search button.
On the search screen, type {location} exactly as given into the search field (hint "Type to search all"); do not reformat or round it.
Tap the first search result whose title matches {location}; do not use the "SHOW ON MAP" button.
On the place panel that opens, tap the star (Favorites) control, not the marker control next to it.
In the Add Favorite dialog, the Name field is pre-filled with the proposed name; leave it unchanged.
Tap Save to confirm the favorite.
