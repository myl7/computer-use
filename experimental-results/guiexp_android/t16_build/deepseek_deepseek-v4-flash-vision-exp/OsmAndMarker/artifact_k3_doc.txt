Open OsmAnd on the map screen.

If a notification shade or expanded panel is open, dismiss it with Back.

Tap the Search button (the magnifier icon) on the map screen.

On the Search panel, focus the field with hint "Type to search all". If it already has text, clear it first.

Type {location} verbatim into that search field; never reformat or round it.

Wait for the search results list to appear.

Tap the first search result whose title matches {location}.

On the place panel that opens, tap the MARKER control — not the favorites star next to it; they write to different stores.

The marker is saved on this tap; no confirmation dialog follows.
