Start on the launcher and open OsmAnd.

If the notification shade or any overlay is open, collapse it or press Back so the map is visible.

On the OsmAnd map screen, tap the Search control.

In the search field, type {location} exactly as given; do not reformat or round it.

Clear any existing text in the search field before typing, and use the real editable text field, not a mislabeled row.

Tap the first search result row to open the place panel.

On the place panel, use the MARKER control, not the favorites star next to it.

Tap the marker; it is saved immediately, with no confirmation dialog.
