Launch the Markor app; the note list screen opens.

On this screen, locate the row whose title exactly matches {file_name}, including its extension.

Do not match by row position; other notes are present and must not be touched.

If the {file_name} row is not visible, scroll the list until it appears.

Long-press the {file_name} row; this selects it and shows the selection top bar.

In the selection top bar, tap the Delete icon — this icon appears only after selection.

A confirmation dialog appears; tap OK to confirm the deletion.

The note is deleted, and the file list screen returns.
