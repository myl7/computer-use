1. On the launcher, tap the Phone app icon.
2. In the dialer, tap "Create new contact" to open the contact editor.
3. In the contact editor, tap the "First name" field.
4. Type the first word of {name} into the "First name" field.
5. Tap the "Last name" field.
6. Type the second word of {name} into the "Last name" field.
7. Scroll down until the "Phone" field is visible.
8. Tap the "Phone" field.
9. Type {number} into the "Phone" field, leaving the phone label at its default.
10. Tap "Save" to finish.
