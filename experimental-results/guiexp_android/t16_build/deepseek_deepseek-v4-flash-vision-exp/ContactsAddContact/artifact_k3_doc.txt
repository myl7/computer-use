Create a new Google Contacts contact for {name} with phone {number}.
Start from the home/launcher; if a panel or shade is open, collapse it or press Back until the launcher is visible.
Open Contacts. If Contacts is not on the launcher, open Phone and go to its Contacts/Favorites area.
On the contacts list or dialer contacts/Favorites screen, locate the create-contact control labeled "Create new contact", "Create contact", or "Add a favorite".
Tap that control to open the contact editor; match it by its label, not by its position.
In the contact editor, tap the "First name" field.
Type the first word of the two-word {name} into "First name"; do not put the full name there.
Tap the "Last name" field.
Type the second word of {name} into "Last name"; do not put the full name there.
If either name field or the phone field is already filled, clear it before typing.
Scroll down in the editor until the phone input is visible.
Tap the editable "Phone" field.
Type {number} exactly as given, preserving every character.
Leave the phone label at its default setting.
If a suggestion list or dialog blocks a field, dismiss or confirm it only as needed to continue.
Scroll if needed to reveal the "Save" button.
Tap "Save" to finish the contact.
Wait for the editor to close and the contacts list, dialer, or previous screen to appear.
If a save-confirmation or account prompt appears, accept the default save action.
Verify the new contact was created if the list is shown.
