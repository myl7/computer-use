1. From the home screen, open the Contacts app (or the Phone app's contacts tab) to reach the contacts list screen.
2. On the contacts list screen, tap the "Create contact" or "Create new contact" button to open the contact editor screen.
3. On the contact editor screen, the name {name} is split: type the first word into the "First name" field.
4. Type the second word into the "Last name" field.
5. Scroll down on the editor until the "Phone" field is visible.
6. Type {number} into the "Phone" field.
7. Leave the phone label at its default.
8. Tap the "Save" button to save the contact and return to the previous screen.
