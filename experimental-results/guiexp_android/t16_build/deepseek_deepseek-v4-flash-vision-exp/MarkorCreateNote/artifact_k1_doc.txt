Open the Markor app to its main screen.
On the main screen, tap the "Create a new file or folder" button.
The create dialog appears. It has a Name field and a small extension field.
Clear the pre-filled Name field (placeholder "my_note").
Enter {file_name} without its extension (the part before the last dot) into the Name field.
Enter the extension (the part after the last dot, including the dot) into the extension field.
Tap OK to confirm and open the editor.
In the editor, tap the text area and type {text}.
Navigate back to save the note.
