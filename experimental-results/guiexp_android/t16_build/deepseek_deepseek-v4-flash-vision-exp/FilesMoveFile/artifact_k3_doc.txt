1. From the launcher, open the Files app.
2. On the Files home screen, tap the hamburger (Show roots) to open the navigation drawer.
3. In the navigation drawer, tap the storage root named sdk_gphone_x86_64.
4. On the storage root list, tap the folder {source_folder}.
5. In the source folder, scroll down until the row labelled exactly {file_name} is visible.
6. Long-press that row to select it.
7. On the selection bar, tap the overflow (three-dot) menu.
8. From the overflow menu, tap "Move to…" to open the destination picker.
9. In the destination picker, tap the hamburger (Show roots) to open its navigation drawer.
10. In the picker's drawer, tap the same storage root sdk_gphone_x86_64.
11. On the storage root list, tap the folder {destination_folder}.
12. In the destination folder view, tap "MOVE" to confirm the move.

Traps:
- The storage root is behind the hamburger, not on the first screen.
- The source folder holds other files; match the row by its exact name.
- The file may be below the visible list; scroll down to find it.
- In the picker, always open the drawer and select the storage root first.
- Use "Move to…", not "Copy to…"; a copy leaves the source file in place and fails.
