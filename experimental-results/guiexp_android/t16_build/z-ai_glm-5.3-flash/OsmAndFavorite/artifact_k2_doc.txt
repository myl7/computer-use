Add a favorite location marker for {location} in the OsmAnd maps app.

1. Open the OsmAnd app and wait for the map screen with its floating round controls.
2. Tap the Search button (magnifier icon) on the map screen to open the search screen.
3. Tap the search field hinted "Type to search all"; if it is pre-filled, clear it first.
4. Type {location} into that field verbatim — never reformat, round, or split it; the same field accepts a place name or a 'lat, lon' pair.
5. Press the keyboard's enter/search key to run the search.
6. If the screen reports nothing was found, tap the "INCREASE SEARCH RADIUS" row, wait, and repeat until results appear.
7. Ignore any transient "Downloading list of available regions" status text; it is not a result row.
8. In the results list, tap the FIRST row; a coordinate query shows a degree-formatted label, a name query shows the place title, so match loosely, never by exact rounded text.
9. Wait for the map to recenter and the place panel with the context menu to appear at the bottom.
10. On the place panel tap the FAVORITES control (the star), NOT the marker/shape control next to it; the two write to different stores and only the star creates the needed favorite.
11. In the add-to-favorites dialog, keep the app-proposed name exactly as offered; do not clear or retype it.
12. Confirm the dialog with its positive button (OK/Save/Add) and wait for it to close.
13. Confirm the star now marks {location} as a favorite; the task is complete.
