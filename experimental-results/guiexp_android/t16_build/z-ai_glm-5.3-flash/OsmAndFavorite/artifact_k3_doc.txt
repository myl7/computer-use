Launch OsmAnd and wait for the map screen (the Search and Configure-map controls must be visible).
Tap the round Search button on the map.
On the search screen, tap the text field with hint "Type to search all".
If the field is pre-filled, tap its Clear button before typing.
Type {location} exactly as given — never reformat, round, or split it; the same field accepts place names and 'lat, lon' pairs.
Press Enter to run the search and wait for the results list.
Trap: if "Could not find anything" with an "INCREASE SEARCH RADIUS" row appears, do not tap it repeatedly; wait briefly for results, or clear and re-enter {location} once.
Ignore any region-download status messages on the screen.
In the results list, tap the FIRST row whose title contains {location}, matching by the row's title text (not by distance or other labels).
The map re-centers and a place panel opens over it.
On the place panel, locate the FAVORITES control: the star icon.
Trap: a marker/flag control sits next to the star; do NOT tap it — it writes to a different store than favorites.
Tap the star to add {location} as a favorite.
A dialog appears proposing a name for the favorite; accept the proposed name unchanged.
Confirm the dialog with Save/OK; the favorite is stored and the task is complete.
