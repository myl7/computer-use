Create a new contact for {name} with phone number {number}.

1. Open the Contacts app (or the Phone/dialer app, then switch to its Contacts tab).
2. On the contact list screen, tap the "Create contact" (add person) action to open the contact editor.
3. In the editor, find the "First name" field and type the FIRST word of {name} only.
4. Find the "Last name" field and type the SECOND word of {name} only. Never put the full {name} into one field.
5. Find the "Phone" field (editable, hint/label "Phone") and type {number} exactly as given.
6. Leave the phone label (Mobile, etc.) at its default; do not change it.
7. Ignore any on-screen "Search contacts" bar — it is not a contact field; do not type into it.
8. Verify by element role, not position: editable fields are the name/phone inputs; dialpad keys and icons are not text fields.
9. Tap the save checkmark/save button to commit the contact.
10. Confirm you are back on the contact list screen; the new contact {name} should appear.
