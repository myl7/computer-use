OPERATION: Create a new Google Contacts contact for {name} with phone {number}

1. Open the Contacts app (may be labeled "Contacts" or "Google Contacts") and wait for the contact list screen.
2. If direct launch fails, open the Phone app and switch to its Contacts view to reach the same list.
3. Verify the list screen is up; an empty list may show "No contacts yet". If neither appears, relaunch and wait.
4. Tap the floating "Create contact" button (found by its content description) to open the contact editor screen.
5. TRAP: the two words of {name} go into two DIFFERENT fields — never type the full name into one field.
6. Tap the "First name" field and enter only the first word of {name}.
7. Tap the "Last name" field and enter only the second word of {name}.
8. Locate every field by its visible hint or label ("First name", "Last name", "Phone"), never by element position; positions shift between screens and sessions.
9. Tap the "Phone" field and enter {number} exactly as given, copied verbatim, once.
10. Leave the phone label at its default; do not open or change the label selector.
11. Locate the "Save" button; if it is not visible, scroll down inside the editor and look again.
12. Tap "Save" and wait for the editor to close.
13. Confirm the contact list screen reappears; this is the success signal.
14. If "Save" is missing, the editor may not have opened — re-tap "Create contact" and refill the fields from step 6.
15. Enter each value exactly once and add no extra spaces, so a repeated attempt cannot duplicate text.
