Add a location marker for {location} in the OsmAnd maps app.

1. Open the OsmAnd app and wait for the map screen to load.
2. Tap the Search button on the map screen to open the search overlay.
3. Tap the search field (hint "Type to search all"); if it already holds text, clear it first with the field's clear (X) control.
4. Type {location} exactly as given — a place name or a "lat, lon" pair — verbatim; never reformat, round, or reword it, the same field accepts both forms.
5. Wait for the results list to appear.
6. If a no-results state appears with an INCREASE SEARCH RADIUS control, tap it and repeat as needed until results show; do not edit or shorten {location}.
7. Tap the first row of the results list, matching it by its title text.
8. On the place panel that opens for the selected place, tap the MARKER control.
9. Do NOT tap the favorites star beside it: the star saves to favorites, while only the marker store counts for this task.
10. The marker is saved immediately on the tap; no confirmation dialog appears, so do not wait for one.
11. Confirm the marker flag is shown on the map at {location}.
