Move the file {file_name} from {source_folder} to {destination_folder} in the Files app (sdk_gphone_x86_64 storage area).

1. Open the Files app; it starts on its home screen, which is NOT the storage area.
2. Tap the "Show roots" hamburger button in the top bar to open the navigation drawer.
3. In the drawer, tap the storage-area entry whose label contains sdk_gphone_x86_64 (the label may be truncated; do not tap any "Files on …" title).
4. In the storage root listing, tap the {source_folder} row (scroll the listing if needed) to open it.
5. If the file row {file_name} is not visible, scroll down until it appears.
6. Long-press the row titled exactly {file_name}, name with extension — the folder holds other files, so match the full title — to enter selection mode.
7. Tap "More options" (three-dot overflow) in the selection bar that appears.
8. Tap "Move to…" in the overflow menu; this cuts the file, and nothing on screen marks the clipboard between now and the paste.
9. A "Move to" picker screen opens; tap "Show roots" there too — the picker starts outside the storage area.
10. In the picker's drawer, tap the sdk_gphone_x86_64 storage entry, skipping any "Files on …" header.
11. In the picker's storage listing, scroll down if needed until {destination_folder} is visible.
12. Tap {destination_folder} to enter it as the destination directory.
13. Tap the "MOVE" confirmation button (never "CANCEL"); nothing is pasted until this final confirmation.
14. Verify afterwards that {file_name} is gone from {source_folder} and present in {destination_folder}; leaving the original behind (a copy) fails the check.
