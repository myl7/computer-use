Open the Files app and wait for its start screen.
Tap the "Show roots" hamburger button to open the navigation drawer; the sdk_gphone_x86_64 storage area is not the start screen.
In the drawer, tap the sdk_gphone_x86_64 storage entry to open its top-level folder list.
Tap the {source_folder} row to open the source folder.
Long-press the row titled exactly {file_name}, matching the full name with extension; the folder holds other files, so do not select a neighboring row.
With the file selected, tap "More options" in the selection bar to open the overflow menu.
Tap "Move to…" — this is the cut half of the move; the app switches to a destination picker screen and nothing on screen marks the clipboard, so trust that the file is staged.
In the picker, tap "Show roots" again to open the drawer; the picker does not start in the storage root either.
Tap the sdk_gphone_x86_64 storage entry inside the picker.
Scroll the folder list down until {destination_folder} becomes visible if it is not already on screen.
Tap the {destination_folder} row to enter it as the destination.
Tap the "MOVE" button to confirm; "CANCEL" aborts without moving.
Do not use "Copy" anywhere — the oracle checks that {file_name} is gone from {source_folder}.
Verify success only after confirming: {file_name} must be present in {destination_folder} and absent from {source_folder}.
