Open Google Contacts.
On the Contacts home screen, tap the Create contact control.
Wait for the contact editor form to appear.
Type the first word of {name} into the First name field.
Type the second word of {name} into the Last name field.
Do not enter the full name into one field.
Tap the Phone field.
Type {number} into the Phone field exactly as given.
Do not add, remove, or reformat characters in the number.
Leave the phone label at its default.
Tap Save.
Return to the contacts list when the editor closes.
