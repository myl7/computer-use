Open Contacts.
On the Contacts home screen, tap Create contact.
On the new contact editor, tap the First name field.
Enter only the first word of {name} in the First name field.
Tap the Last name field.
Enter only the second word of {name} in the Last name field.
Tap the Phone field.
Enter {number} in the Phone field.
Do not combine {name} into one field; split it across First name and Last name.
Do not change the phone label; leave it at its default value.
Tap Save.
Return to the Contacts home screen and confirm the contact was created.
