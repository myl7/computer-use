Open the Contacts app.
On the Contacts home screen, tap Create contact to open the new contact editor.
On the new contact editor, tap the First name field.
Enter only the first word of {name}.
Tap the Last name field.
Enter only the second word of {name}.
Do not enter {name} as a single value in one field.
Tap the Phone field.
Enter {number} exactly as provided.
Leave the phone label at its default value.
Tap Save to return to the Contacts home screen.
