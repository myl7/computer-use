Open Contacts.
On the Contacts home screen, tap Create contact.
On the New contact editor, split {name} into two words.
Tap the First name field.
Enter the first word of {name}.
Tap the Last name field.
Enter the second word of {name}.
Tap the Phone field.
Enter {number} verbatim.
Leave the phone label at its default.
Tap Save to return to the Contacts home screen.
