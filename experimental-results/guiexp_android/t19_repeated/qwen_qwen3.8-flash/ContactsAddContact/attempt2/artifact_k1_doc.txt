Open the Contacts app.
Wait for the Contacts home screen.
Tap Create contact.
Wait for the contact editor screen.
Use {name} as two separate values: the first word and the second word.
Enter the first word of {name} into the First name field.
Enter the second word of {name} into the Last name field.
Enter {number} into the Phone field.
Leave the phone label at its default.
Tap Save.
Wait for the Contacts home screen to show the new contact.
