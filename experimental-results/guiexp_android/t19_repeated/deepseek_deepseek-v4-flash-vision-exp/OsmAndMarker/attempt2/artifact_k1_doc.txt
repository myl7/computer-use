1. Home screen: tap the OsmAnd app icon to open it.
2. Map screen: tap the search field at the top.
3. Search screen: type {location} verbatim into the search field.
4. Search results screen: tap the first result row.
5. Place panel: tap the MARKER control, not the favorites star next to it.
6. No confirmation dialog follows; the marker is saved on the tap.
7. The search field accepts both place names and coordinates; never reformat or round {location}.
8. The first result is the one to tap; do not match by title or other criteria.
9. The favorites star writes to a different store; only MARKER is read by the oracle.
10. If the map screen shows a "North is up" button, ignore it; it is not the search.
11. If the home screen has no scrollable content, do not scroll; just find the OsmAnd icon.
