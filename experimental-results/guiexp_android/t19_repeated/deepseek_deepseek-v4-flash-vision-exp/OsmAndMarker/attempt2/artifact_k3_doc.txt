1. On the launcher, tap the OsmAnd icon to open the map screen.
2. If a notification shade is open, tap the Collapse control to dismiss it.
3. On the map screen, tap the Search magnifier button to open the search screen.
4. In the search field (hint "Type to search all"), clear any pre-filled text before typing.
5. Tap the search field to focus it, then type {location} verbatim as one string — do not split, reformat, or round it.
6. Press Enter on the keyboard to run the search.
7. On the search results list, tap the first row whose title matches {location}.
8. On the place panel, tap "SHOW ON MAP" to center the place on the map.
9. On the place panel, tap the MARKER control — not the favorites star next to it; the star writes to a different store.
10. The marker saves immediately on tap; no confirmation dialog follows.
