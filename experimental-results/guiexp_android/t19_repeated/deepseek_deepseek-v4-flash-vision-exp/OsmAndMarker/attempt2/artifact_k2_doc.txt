From the home screen, open the OsmAnd app.
If a notification shade is open, dismiss it and return to the map.
On the map screen, tap the search button to open the search field.
If the search field already contains text, clear it.
Type {location} exactly as given into the search field; do not reformat or round it.
Wait for the search results list to appear.
Tap the first search result row.
On the place panel, tap the MARKER control, not the favorites star.
The marker is saved immediately; no confirmation dialog appears.
