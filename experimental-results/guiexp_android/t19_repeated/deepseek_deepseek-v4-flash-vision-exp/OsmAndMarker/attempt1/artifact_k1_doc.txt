From the home screen, open the OsmAnd app.

On the map screen, tap the search icon to open the search field.

Type {location} into the search field exactly as given. Do not reformat, round, or split a coordinate pair.

Wait for the search results to load.

Tap the first search result in the list.

On the place panel that appears, tap the MARKER control. Do not tap the favorites star next to it—the marker and the favorite write to different stores.

The marker is saved immediately on that tap; no confirmation dialog follows.
