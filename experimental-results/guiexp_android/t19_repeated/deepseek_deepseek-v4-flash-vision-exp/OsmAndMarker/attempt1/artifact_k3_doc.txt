1. Open OsmAnd from the launcher.
2. If the notification shade is open, collapse it to return to the map.
3. On the map screen, tap the Search button.
4. On the search screen, tap the search field and clear any pre-filled text.
5. Type {location} verbatim into the search field; it accepts both place names and coordinate pairs.
6. On the search results screen, tap the first result whose title matches {location}.
7. On the place panel, tap the MARKER control.
8. Do not tap the favorites star next to it.
9. The marker is stored on the tap; no confirmation dialog follows.
