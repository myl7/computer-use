Open Markor.

On the file list screen, find the note row whose title is exactly {file_name}. Do not match by row position; other notes are present. If the row is not visible, scroll the list until it appears.

Long-press that row to open its context menu.

In the context menu, tap "Delete".

A confirmation dialog appears. Tap "OK" to confirm the deletion.

The note is now deleted. Leave all other notes untouched.
