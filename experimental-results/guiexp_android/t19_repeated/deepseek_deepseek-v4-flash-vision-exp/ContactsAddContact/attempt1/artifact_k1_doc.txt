Open the Phone app from the launcher.
Tap the "Create new contact" option in the Phone app.
Wait for the Google Contacts contact editor screen.
Tap the "First name" field.
Enter the first word of {name} into First name.
Tap the "Last name" field.
Enter the second word of {name} into Last name.
Do not enter the full {name} into one field; first and last are separate.
Scroll down until the phone number field is visible.
Tap the field labeled "Phone".
Enter {number} exactly as given, verbatim.
Leave the phone number label at its default value.
Tap "Save" to store the new contact.
Wait for the editor to close or the contact to be saved.
