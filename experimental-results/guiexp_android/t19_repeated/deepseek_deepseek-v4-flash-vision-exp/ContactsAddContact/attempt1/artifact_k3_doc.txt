Open the Contacts app (or the Phone app, then tap "Create new contact").
Tap the "Create new contact" button to open the contact editor.
In the "First name" field, type the first word of {name}.
In the "Last name" field, type the second word of {name}.
If the phone field is not visible, scroll down until it appears.
In the "Phone" field, type {number}.
Leave the phone label at its default setting.
Tap "Save" to create the contact.

Trap: {name} is exactly two words — split it on the space, never type the whole name into one field.
Trap: The phone field may be below the fold; scroll down to reveal it before typing.
Trap: The "Save" button is always present after filling the form; do not navigate back without saving.
