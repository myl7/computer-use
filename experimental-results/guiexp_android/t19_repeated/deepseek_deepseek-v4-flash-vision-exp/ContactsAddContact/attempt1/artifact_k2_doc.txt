Launch the Contacts app from the launcher.  
If the Contacts screen has no "Create contact" button, open the Phone app instead and tap "Create new contact".  
On the contact editor, enter the first word of {name} into the "First name" field.  
Enter the second word of {name} into the "Last name" field — the name has exactly two words, so never type the whole name into one field.  
Scroll down until the "Phone" field is visible.  
Tap the "Phone" field and type {number}; leave the phone label at its default.  
Tap "Save" to finish.
