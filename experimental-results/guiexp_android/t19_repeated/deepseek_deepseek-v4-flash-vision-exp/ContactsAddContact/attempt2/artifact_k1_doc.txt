Start on the Android launcher.
Open the Phone app by tapping its Phone icon.
On the Phone screen, tap "Create new contact" to open the Google Contacts contact editor.
If the contact editor is not shown, open the Contacts app and choose "Create contact".
In the contact editor, find the "First name" field.
Tap the "First name" field.
Enter only the first word of {name}.
Find the "Last name" field.
Tap the "Last name" field.
Enter only the second word of {name}.
Do not put the full {name} into either name field.
Scroll down in the contact editor until the phone number field is visible.
Tap the "Phone" number field.
Enter {number} exactly as given, with no changes.
Leave the phone label at its default setting.
If needed, scroll down to reveal the Save control.
Tap "Save" to store the new contact.
Wait for the editor to close and the contact to be saved.
