Open the Contacts app from the launcher.
On the contacts list, tap the "Create contact" entry (match it by title; if using the Phone app, tap "Create new contact" instead).
The contact editor opens.
Tap the "First name" field and type the first word of {name}.
Tap the "Last name" field and type the second word of {name}.
The name must be split at the single space: the first word goes only into First name, the second only into Last name.
Scroll down until the "Phone" field is visible.
Tap the "Phone" field and type {number}.
Leave the phone label at its default value.
Tap "Save" to save the contact and return to the previous screen.
