Open the Contacts app from the launcher.
If you are in the Dialer instead, tap the Favorites tab, then tap "Add a favorite" to open the contact editor.
In the Contacts app, tap the button that creates a new contact (labeled "Create new contact" or "Create contact").
In the contact editor, tap the "First name" field and type the first word of {name}.
Tap the "Last name" field and type the second word of {name}. Do not type the full name into one field.
Scroll down until the "Phone" field is visible.
Tap the "Phone" field and type {number}.
Leave the phone label at its default.
Tap "Save" to save the contact.
