Open the app 'Simple Calendar Pro' and wait for its main screen.
Tap the floating 'New Event' button; if the event editor does not open, tap 'New Event' again.
On the event editor, tap the 'Title' field and type {event_title}.
Tap the 'Description' field (directly below Title) and type {event_description}.
Tap the start-date row, a TextView showing a date like 'Month D (Weekday)', to open the date-picker dialog.
In the picker, tap the day cell whose description reads '{day} {MonthName} {year}'; match month and year too, because the same day number appears in every month, and scroll months if needed.
Confirm the date picker with 'OK' — every picker dialog must be confirmed with 'OK' or the change is discarded.
Tap the start-time row, the first HH:MM TextView on the form, to open the time-picker dialog.
Tap the hour cell described '{hour} hours' (24-hour clock; try a zero-padded label if not found).
Leave the minutes at 00 and confirm the time picker with 'OK'.
Tap the end-time row, the second HH:MM TextView below the start time — do not reopen the start-time row.
There is no duration field: the end time must equal the start time plus {duration_mins} minutes.
In the time picker, tap the minutes selector (the view reading '0 minutes') to switch from the hours list to the minutes list.
Tap the minutes cell described '{duration_mins} minutes'; scroll down if it is off-screen.
Confirm the time picker with 'OK'.
Tap the 'Save' button and verify you return to the main screen with the new event listed.
