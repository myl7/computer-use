Add a location marker for {location} in the OsmAnd maps app.

1. Open the OsmAnd app and wait for its map screen to load.
2. Tap the Search button (magnifier icon) on the map screen to open the search overlay.
3. Tap the search field showing the hint "Type to search all"; if it already contains text, first tap its Clear (X) button to empty it.
4. Type {location} VERBATIM into this one field — never reformat, round, or split it; it accepts both place names and "lat, lon" coordinate pairs as-is.
5. Wait for the results list to appear below the field.
6. If a "no results" state with an INCREASE SEARCH RADIUS label appears, do not keep widening the radius — clear the field, retype {location} exactly as given, and search again.
7. Tap the FIRST result row whose text contains {location}; for coordinates the row may show them reformatted (e.g., as degrees with N/E letters), so match by the numbers in the row title — never tap the search input itself.
8. A place panel for the selected point opens over the map.
9. On that panel, tap the control labeled "Marker".
10. Do NOT tap the favorites/star icon next to it — favorites and markers are separate stores, and only the marker counts for this task.
11. The marker is saved at the moment of the tap; no confirmation dialog appears — the task is complete.
