Open the OsmAnd app and wait for its map screen to load.
On the map screen, tap the Search button (magnifier icon).
On the search screen, tap the text field hinted "Type to search all".
Type {location} exactly as given — never reformat, round, or split it; the same field accepts place names and "lat, lon" pairs.
Wait for the results list to appear; do NOT tap "INCREASE SEARCH RADIUS" or repeat-tap it.
If a "Could not find anything" no-results state appears, tap the Clear (X) button in the search field and retype {location} verbatim.
In the results list, tap the FIRST row whose title matches {location}.
For coordinates, OsmAnd reformats them as degrees with N/S/E/W letters — match the row by its numbers, not by the exact typed string.
Match only non-editable result rows; the search field itself also echoes the text and must be ignored.
On the place panel that opens for the selected location, tap the MARKER control.
Do NOT tap the favorites/star control next to it — it writes to a different store and will not satisfy the task.
No confirmation dialog follows: the marker is saved at the moment of the tap.
Optionally close the panel and confirm the marker flag now sits on the map at {location}.
