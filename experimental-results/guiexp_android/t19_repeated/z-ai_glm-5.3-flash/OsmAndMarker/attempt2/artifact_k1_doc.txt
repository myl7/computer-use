TASK: Add a location marker for {location} in the OsmAnd maps app.

1. Open the OsmAnd app and wait for the map screen.
2. Tap the Search (magnifier) button on the map to open the search overlay.
3. If the search field is pre-filled with old text, tap its Clear (X) button to empty it first.
4. Tap the search field (hint "Type to search all") and type {location} VERBATIM — never reformat, round, or split a "lat, lon" pair, and never substitute a different or nearby place name.
5. Wait for the results list to populate.
6. If "INCREASE SEARCH RADIUS" appears, tap it at most once; do not keep tapping it in a loop.
7. If results still do not appear, tap Clear, retype {location} exactly as given, and wait again.
8. Tap the FIRST result row, matched by its title text matching {location}.
9. The place panel slides in for the selected place.
10. On the place panel, tap the MARKER control (marker/flag icon, "Add marker").
11. TRAP: do NOT tap the favorites star next to the marker control — the star saves a favorite, not a marker, and only markers count for this task.
12. The marker is stored on that single tap; no confirmation dialog appears and none is needed.
13. Close the panel if desired; the marker pin on the map means the task is complete.
