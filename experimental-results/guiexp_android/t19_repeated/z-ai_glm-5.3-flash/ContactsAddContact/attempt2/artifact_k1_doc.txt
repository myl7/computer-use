OPERATION: Create a new contact in Google Contacts (Android)
GOAL: Create a new contact for {name}. Their number is {number}.
SCREENS IN ORDER: Contacts list → New-contact editor → Contacts list.

1. Open the Contacts app and wait for the contacts list screen.
2. Tap the "Create contact" floating action button to open the new-contact editor.
3. Tap the "First name" field and type the first word of {name}.
4. Tap the "Last name" field and type the second word of {name}.
5. Tap the "Phone" field and type {number} exactly as given.
6. Leave the phone label next to the Phone field at its default; do not change it.
7. Tap the Save (checkmark) control at the top of the editor.
8. Confirm the editor closes and the contacts list is shown again with the new contact.

TRAPS:
- {name} has exactly two words and must be SPLIT: first word into "First name", second word into "Last name". Never type the full name into a single field.
- The editor shows several fields; fill only First name, Last name, and Phone, ignoring all other optional fields.
- Do not modify the phone label selector next to the Phone field; keep its default value.
- If the list screen shows "No contacts yet", the Create contact button is still present — tap it as usual.
