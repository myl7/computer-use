GOAL: Create a new contact named {name} whose phone number is {number} in Google Contacts.

1. Open the Contacts app and wait for the contacts list screen.
2. On the contacts list, tap the floating "Create contact" button, found by its "Create contact" label, not by position.
3. Wait for the contact editor screen showing the First name, Last name, and Phone fields.
4. Tap the "First name" field and type the FIRST word of {name}.
5. Tap the "Last name" field and type the SECOND word of {name}.
6. Tap the "Phone" field and type {number} exactly as given.
7. Leave the phone label at its default; do not open or change the label selector.
8. Locate the "Save" button on the editor; if it is not visible, scroll down and look again.
9. Tap "Save" and wait; the app returns to the contacts list once the contact is stored.
10. Confirm the contact {name} appears in the list; if the editor is still open, the save did not register, so tap "Save" again.

TRAPS:
- The name is split across TWO fields: first word of {name} goes to "First name", second word to "Last name". Never type the full name into one field.
- Identify every field by its hint or label text ("First name", "Last name", "Phone") together with being editable, never by element index; indexes shift between screens and runs.
- Do not type into the search bar or any dialer or dialpad control; only the editor's First name, Last name, and Phone fields receive text.
- If any editor field already contains text, clear it before typing the value.
- The Save button can sit below the fold; scroll the editor if it is not on screen.
