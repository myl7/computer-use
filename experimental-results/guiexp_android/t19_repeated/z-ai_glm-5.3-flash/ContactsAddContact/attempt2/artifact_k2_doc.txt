TASK: Create a new contact for {name}. Their number is {number}.

1. Open the Contacts app and wait for the contacts list screen.
2. Tap the "Create contact" button (plus icon) on the list screen; the contact editor screen opens.
3. On the editor screen, tap the field labeled "First name" and type only the FIRST word of {name}.
4. Tap the field labeled "Last name" and type only the SECOND word of {name}.
5. Tap the field labeled "Phone" and type {number} exactly as given, without reformatting.
6. Leave the phone label selector at its default value; do not open or change it.
7. Tap the Save control (checkmark icon in the top bar). The editor closes and the app returns to the previous list screen.
8. Confirm the contact for {name} now exists; if a confirmation dialog appears during save, confirm it.

TRAPS:
- The name is split across TWO fields: first word of {name} goes to "First name", second word to "Last name". Never type the full name into one field.
- Do not type into the search bar on the contacts list; it is not a contact field and the editor fields are elsewhere.
- Identify editor fields by their labels ("First name", "Last name", "Phone"), never by on-screen position, which shifts between launches.
- The number must be typed verbatim into "Phone"; a separate phone-label dropdown sits nearby and must stay untouched.
- If the editor is still open after tapping Save, the tap did not register; locate the checkmark again and tap it once more.
