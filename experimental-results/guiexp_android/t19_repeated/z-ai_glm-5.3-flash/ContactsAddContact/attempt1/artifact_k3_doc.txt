OPERATION: Create a contact for {name} with phone number {number} (Google Contacts, Android).

1. Open the Contacts app and wait for the contact list screen.
2. If the session starts on the Phone/dialer app instead, that is fine; the same create control leads onward.
3. On the list screen, tap the "Create contact" floating button.
4. TRAP: do not type {name} or {number} into the "Search contacts" bar — that only filters the list, it does not create anything.
5. Wait for the contact editor screen showing "First name", "Last name", and "Phone" fields.
6. Locate each field by its visible hint/label text; their order and positions change between runs, so never rely on positions.
7. Tap the "First name" field and type the FIRST word of {name}.
8. Tap the "Last name" field and type the SECOND word of {name}.
9. TRAP: {name} is two words and must be split across these two separate fields; never enter the full name into one field.
10. Tap the "Phone" field and type {number} exactly as given.
11. TRAP: leave the phone label (e.g. "Mobile") at its default selection; do not open or change it.
12. If any dialog appears on the editor, dismiss or confirm it so all three fields remain filled.
13. Find the "Save" button; if it is not visible, scroll down the editor and look again.
14. Tap "Save".
15. Success check: the editor closes and the app returns to the contact list showing {name}; if still in the editor, locate "Save" again and tap it.
