GOAL: Create a new contact for {name}. Their number is {number}.

1. Open the Contacts app and wait for the contacts list screen.
2. Tap the "Create contact" floating action button; the create-contact form opens.
3. The form has separate "First name" and "Last name" fields — {name} must be split across them.
4. Tap the "First name" field and type only the FIRST word of {name}.
5. Tap the "Last name" field and type only the SECOND word of {name}.
6. Never type the full {name} into a single field; the form has no full-name field.
7. Tap the "Phone" field and type {number} exactly as given, verbatim.
8. Leave the phone label selector next to the Phone field at its default; do not change it.
9. Fill only these three fields; skip every other optional field on the form.
10. Tap "Save" to commit the contact.
11. Confirm the app returns to the contacts list and the new contact {name} is shown.
