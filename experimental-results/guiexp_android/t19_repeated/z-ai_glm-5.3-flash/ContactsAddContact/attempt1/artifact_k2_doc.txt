Open the Contacts app (if the phone instead shows the dialer, switch to its Contacts tab first).
On the contacts list screen, tap the floating "Create contact" button; the contact editor screen opens.
In the editor, tap the "First name" field and type the first word of {name}.
Tap the "Last name" field and type the second word of {name}.
Tap the "Phone" field and type {number} exactly as given.
Leave the phone label at its default (Mobile); do not change it.
Tap the Save control (checkmark icon) at the top of the editor.
Confirm the editor closes and the contacts list (or dialer contacts tab) is shown again, with the new contact present.
TRAPS:
The contact name must be split: first word into "First name", second word into "Last name" — never type the full {name} into one field.
Do not type into the "Search contacts" bar; it is the only other editable field and typing there searches instead of creating.
Do not type on a dialpad if the dialer is open; reach the contact editor before entering any values.
The Phone field is a separate field from the name fields; {number} goes only there.
The Save control is the top checkmark icon, not the back button; pressing back discards the contact.
If a "save to account/device" choice appears, accept the default to finish saving.
