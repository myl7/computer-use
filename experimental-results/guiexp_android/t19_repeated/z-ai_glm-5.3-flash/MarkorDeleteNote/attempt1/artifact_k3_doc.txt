OPERATION: Delete a note in Markor

1. Launch the Markor app and wait for its main file-list screen to load.
2. Verify the main screen is showing (app title "Markor" and the new-file button are visible); if not, go home and relaunch Markor.
3. In the file list, locate the row whose title is exactly {file_name} (name plus extension).
4. Never pick a row by position — several other notes are listed and must be left untouched.
5. If no row titled {file_name} is visible, scroll the list down and look again; repeat as needed.
6. Long-press the row titled {file_name} to enter selection mode.
7. Verify the selection top bar has appeared with the target row selected; do not proceed otherwise.
8. Tap the Delete icon shown in the selection top bar.
9. A "Confirm Delete" dialog appears; its message should reference {file_name}.
10. If the dialog does not name {file_name}, dismiss it and reselect the correct row.
11. Tap OK in the confirmation dialog.
12. Verify {file_name} no longer appears in the file list; all other notes must remain.

TRAPS:
- Match rows only by the exact title {file_name}, including the extension.
- The target row can be off-screen; scroll until it appears before long-pressing.
- Deletion only works on a selection: the long-press must succeed before the Delete icon exists.
- The delete is not final until OK is tapped in the "Confirm Delete" dialog.
